# MindBridge AI

### An AI-Powered Mental Health Support and Early Intervention Platform

MindBridge AI is an early-stage innovation designed to improve access to mental health awareness, emotional well-being information and early support, particularly for underserved communities in Nigeria and Africa.

## Our Mission

To leverage artificial intelligence to make mental health information and supportive guidance more accessible, affordable and inclusive.

## Key Features

1. AI Mental Health Assistant
2. Emotional Well-being Check-ins
3. Anxiety Awareness
4. Depression Awareness
5. Stress Management Guidance
6. Mental Health Education
7. Human Support Recommendations

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Google Gemini API
- GitHub
- Google AI

## Target Beneficiaries

- Young people
- Students
- Women
- Underserved communities
- Low-income individuals

## Development Stage

Idea stage / Early Prototype.

## Future Development

- Hausa language support
- Additional Nigerian languages
- Improved AI conversations
- Verified mental health referral directory
- Improved privacy and security
- Mobile-friendly experience

## Important Safety Notice

MindBridge AI is an educational prototype.

It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional mental health services.

It is not an emergency service.

Users experiencing serious distress should seek qualified human support.
